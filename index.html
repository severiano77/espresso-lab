☕ About Espresso Lab

Making great espresso at home can feel confusing with so many machines, grinders, and techniques out there. Espresso Lab exists to cut through the noise with practical, step-by-step guides and honest gear evaluations so you can enjoy cafe-quality coffee in your own kitchen.

💡 Quick Barista Tip

Always weigh your coffee grounds and liquid yield! A 0.1g digital scale is the fastest way to get consistent tasting espresso every morning.
